# CARD v4.12.0 — 2026-11-25

> Minor release on top of v4.11.1: three new company settings, all off by default — loads on daily logs (FR-188, PR
> #315), a second person for transports entered from evidence (FR-189, PR #316) and partner-owned equipment in the
> suppliers summary (FR-190, PR #317) — with their design documents (issues 44, 52, 300). Backward compatible: no
> patch; six new columns with default 0; no role, DocPerm or accounting change; no new whitelisted method.
> **Published 2026-11-25** (tag `v4.12.0`, owner approval 2026-11-25). **Published ≠ deployed** (see
> [DEPLOYMENTS.md](DEPLOYMENTS.md)).
> Labels: **Verified / Partially Verified / Unknown / Not Supported / Deprecated / Removed**.

- **Version:** 4.12.0 (`card/__init__.py`, `__version__ = "4.12.0"`).

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- The three settings are off after the upgrade; each is a company decision.
- FR-188: CARD Mobile has no load fields yet; a new daily log shows them on Desk after its first save.
- FR-189: a delegate of the enterer may still submit; a transport typed without any intake field is not covered.
- FR-190: a count and a card net only; no partner entitlement or statement.
- Not yet seen on a real Desk (source and CI tests only).
- v4.11.1 is not deployed: its Production upgrade of 2026-11-25 was rolled back in its window.
- Everything listed for v4.11.1 that this page does not change.

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.11.1 or v4.10.1:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate`; `bench --site <site> clear-cache`;
  restart. No `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/` change).
- **What migrate writes:** six new columns with default 0 (three on Card Company Settings, two on Card Daily Log Row,
  one on Equipment Transport) and their DocType records. From v4.10.1 it also does what the v4.11.1 page says. **It
  does not write:** any document, amount, GL entry, role or permission; no Accounting Dimension.
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner):** see the v4.11.1 page; then check in Card Settings > Company settings that
  the three new switches exist and are off.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Code only, to the version the site ran before. The
  new columns stay and are ignored by older code.
