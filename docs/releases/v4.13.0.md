> Minor release on top of v4.12.2. One rule changes: a Monthly Card is approved only after its month ends, and an
> approved card of an open month can be reopened on its own number (FR-191). Two additions: the maintenance payer on

> DocPerm change; CARD posts no accounting entry.

## 3. Added — Partially Verified

- **FR-192 — Maintenance charged to.** Master data on the equipment; no effect on amounts, invoices or reports.
- **FR-193 — Project Invoice Profitability.** Roles: System Manager and Accounts Manager (as Equipment Partial
  Margin). It is revenue minus purchase-invoice expenses, not a full net profit: no payroll, depreciation, overhead
  or journal-entry costs.
- **Four whitelisted methods of FR-191:** `card.approval_period.get_approval_eligibility` (GET),
  `card.open_month.reopen_card`, `approve_open_month_card`, `reopen_open_month_cards` (POST).

Verified by CI tests. Not opened on a real Desk before the release.

## 9. Breaking Changes — Verified

- **Behaviour change (FR-191):** a Monthly Card of a month that has not ended cannot be submitted, by any role and on
  any route. Sites whose users approve cards during the month must change that habit: save during the month, approve
  from the first day of the next month. Cards approved before the upgrade stay approved.

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- With an ACTIVE CARD monthly card Workflow, Frappe refuses the reopen and the final approval of an open card (no
  native transition). Activating the Workflow needs a follow-up decision for the open-month path.
- The end of a month never approves a card: the final approval is a manual action.
- FR-193 counts every Purchase Invoice line of a project, stock and asset purchases too. Its total row is rounded
  from the unrounded total and can differ from the sum of the shown rows by one unit of the last decimal.
- FR-192 is never filled automatically, also for owned equipment.
- None of the three features was seen on a real Desk before the release (source and CI tests only).
- Everything listed for v4.12.2 that this page does not change.

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.12.2:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate`; `bench --site <site> clear-cache`; restart. No
  `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/` change).
- **What migrate writes:** the DocType records of Card Monthly Card, its two child tables and Card Equipment (field
  properties; one new column `tabCard Equipment`.`maintenance_on`); the Report record and the sidebar link of
  FR-193; the patch `card.patches.v4_13_0.open_month_workflow_state` (one Workflow State; rows of that state on the
  CARD monthly card Workflow when it exists; the Workflow is not activated). **It does not write:** any card,
  amount, GL entry, role or permission; no Accounting Dimension.
- **No card changes on upgrade.** Reopening the cards of an open month is a separate, explicit action of a System
  Manager (`reopen_open_month_cards`, dry run first).
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner):** try to approve a card of the current month (refused, with the earliest
  date); open an equipment and see "Maintenance charged to"; open Project Invoice Profitability for a period.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Code only, to v4.12.2. The column, the Workflow
  State and its Workflow rows stay (harmless). A card that is in the state "Open – month not ended" at the rollback
  is a submitted card the previous code treats as not approved in no special way: return such cards to "Approved"
  with `approve_open_month_card` before a rollback, or restore from the backup.
