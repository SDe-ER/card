# CARD v4.1.2 — 2026-10-25

> Patch release on top of v4.1.1: complete launcher navigation on upgraded sites, a simpler
> Equipment Transport form, and language consistency for stored Arabic values. No new patch; one
> idempotent `after_migrate` hook.
> Labels: **Verified / Partially Verified / Unknown / Not Supported / Deprecated / Removed**.

## 1. Version Overview

On a site that saved its own CARD dock layer before v3.0.0 shipped the launcher, frappe shows only
the entries that layer names: on production the dock rail showed one entry ("Card (Custom)") and
`/desk/card` only the dashboard sidebar's three items (dashboard, monthly cards, transports); it hid Equipment, Movement & Transport, Operating Units, Operation Cards, Expenses,
Materials, Suppliers & Customers, Reports and Settings. v4.1.2 restores those entries on such layers.
It also reorders the Equipment Transport form around the operational steps and translates the
stored Arabic values for English users. No accounting, permission or data change.

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- Stored Arabic values stay Arabic for languages CARD ships no locale for, and where frappe shows a

  CARD documents (Material Requests, Stock Entries, invoices) are drafts.

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Verified

`bench --site <site> migrate` runs no new patch; its `after_migrate` hook
`card.navigation.restore_launcher_entries` writes only non-standard `Dock` layers of the app `card` whose `modified` is older than the
shipped dock file; it appends rows and never deletes. A second migrate changes nothing.

Rollback: v4.1.1 code runs on the migrated data (the hook only adds dock rows; the DocType change
adds no column). To undo the navigation change only, remove the rows in Manage Dock, or restore
the pre-upgrade backup.
