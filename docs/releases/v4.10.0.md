## 3. Added

- **FR-170 — Owner-paid cash payments.** New DocType `Card Cash Disbursement` (company-scoped, `track_changes`). A
  cash row of a Draft Monthly Card is requested with its evidence, approved by a Card Manager who is not the requester,
  queued, and posted by a dedicated service identity as one submitted Journal Entry (Dr the Supplier payable, Cr the
  validated Cash account, on the payment date). Controlled cancellation (request with a reason, then the Journal Entry
  cancelled in ERPNext) and one replacement per cancelled payment. An approved payment that was never posted is
  cancelled with a reason. New whitelisted endpoints in `card.cash_disbursement` (all with explicit `methods`):
  `request_cash_disbursement`, `approve_cash_disbursement`, `queue_posting`, `run_posting_queue`,
  `request_cancellation`, `request_replacement` (POST) and `get_cash_row_status`, `get_cash_disbursements` (GET).
  PR #277 (merged `b23df6f`). Verified (tests in CI; QA evidence in the PR).
- **FR-169 — Dated additions and deductions.** New required field `adjustment_date` on `Card Monthly Card Adjustment`,
  inside the card period. Patch `v4_10_0.backfill_adjustment_date`. PR #277. Verified.
- **FR-171 — Settlement shared components and the kept supplier.** Beneficiary resolution, posting gate, transaction
  identity and payment-date checks; the card keeps its supplier; correction with `supplier_correction_reason`. PR
  #277. Verified.
- **FR-168 — CARD workspace dashboard.** Operations dashboard with the month's financial figures, the cards that need
  review, the monthly values chart and grouped links. PR #273 (merged `5f0ef52`). Verified.

## 9. Breaking Changes — Verified

These apply by default to every site that migrates to v4.10.0. Full wording and request formats are in
`CHANGELOG.md`, section `[4.10.0]`, "Breaking changes".

- **FR-169 — `adjustment_date` is required.** A write of a `Card Monthly Card` with an adjustment row that has no
  date, or a date outside `from_date`..`to_date`, is refused with HTTP 417 (`MandatoryError` or a validation error).
  Affected: `POST`/`PUT /api/resource/Card Monthly Card`, `POST`/`PATCH /api/v2/document/Card Monthly Card`,
  `frappe.client.insert`, `frappe.client.save`, `frappe.client.set_value` on a row, and **Data Import** of cards with
  adjustment rows (the column "Adjustment date" is required). No server default and no fallback. Fix: send
  `"adjustment_date": "YYYY-MM-DD"` inside the card period on every row. Reads are unchanged. Verified in
  `card/adjustments.py` (`check_adjustment_date`) and the DocType JSON.
- **FR-171 — supplier changes.** A save that changes a saved Draft card's `supplier` without
  `supplier_correction_reason`, or sets an arbitrary supplier, is refused. Verified in `card/settlement.py` and the
  card controller.
- **FR-168 — money methods.** `customer_value_last_month`, `supplier_cost_last_month`, `internal_value_last_month`
  and `chart_data` return no figure (instead of zeros) to a user without money read. Verified (PR #273).
- **FR-170 — cash rows and cancellation.** A cash row is excluded from the card totals and the invoice. A row with a
  live cash payment cannot be removed, unticked or changed in amount, Cash account, kind, type or side, and a card with
  a live cash payment cannot be cancelled (HTTP 417 on the save or cancel). Verified in `card_monthly_card.py`.
- **Journal Entry hooks.** `before_cancel` and `on_cancel` of every Journal Entry now call CARD; they act only on an
  entry linked to a cash payment (its cancellation needs a recorded cancellation request). Other entries are unchanged.
  Verified in `card/hooks.py` and `card/cash_disbursement.py`.

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- **Cash posting is not active after deployment.** The role `Card Cash Posting Service` is not shipped. Posting needs
  a dedicated service user with only that role, explicit Company User Permissions (start with one company) and a
  runner (a host job calling `run_posting_queue` with token authentication). Until then the executor fails closed.
  Card Managers can still queue an approved payment: it stays in Posting (nothing is lost; it can be cancelled with a
  reason). Tell users not to queue payments until posting is enabled.
- The executor reads a company grant from User Permissions and does not check `applicable_for` (follow-up before the
  service identity is created).
- Review the ERPNext Notifications on Journal Entry before posting is enabled (bulk postings can send e-mails).
- CARD statements exclude cash rows, also after posting; the deduction appears in the ERPNext ledger (owner decision
  2026-11-16).
- The new FR-170 messages added in the last fixes are translated in Arabic only; other locales show English.
- External REST clients and Data Import templates that write adjustment rows are **Unknown** (not verified on any
  site).

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

### Patches and migrate effects (from v4.9.0)

- **New patch:** `card.patches.v4_10_0.backfill_adjustment_date` under `[post_model_sync]`. It reloads the doctype,
  then fills only empty dates: the card's `from_date`, or the first day of its year and month. It writes no amount,
  side, total or modified time. Idempotent (a second migrate changes nothing). Verified by the CI upgrade rehearsal
  (v1.0.1 -> candidate: legacy rows dated, second migrate unchanged, a legacy Draft card saves) and by a
  historical-schema rehearsal on an isolated QA site (51 rows on 37 cards; 26 Draft cards saved unchanged).
- **DocType sync:** new DocType `Card Cash Disbursement`; new fields on `Card Monthly Card Adjustment` and
  `Card Monthly Card` (CHANGELOG `[4.10.0]`, Migration).
- **Fixtures:** the Card workspace and its sidebar are re-imported (FR-168).
- **No accounting document is created by migrate.**

### Upgrade steps (for the operator; run on a verified backup)

1. Check that no integration or Data Import template writes adjustment rows without a date (section 9).
2. Verify the backup (database and files) before the change, per the policy.
3. Rehearse the upgrade on a copy of the site's data (v4.9.0 -> v4.10.0) before Production.
4. Deploy the v4.10.0 image or code. Do **not** create the posting service identity or the runner.
5. Run `bench migrate` once, then again; the second run must make no change.
6. Check: every legacy adjustment row has a date; submitted-card amounts identical before and after; no Journal Entry
   created; the workspace loads; posting and ZATCA unchanged.

### Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested

The rollback target is **v4.9.0, commit `660633b`** (the last recorded Production version). A rollback restores the
**code only**. It does not restore the database.

- **Stored amounts:** no amount is changed by v4.10.0's migrate; submitted cards keep their stored totals.
- **`adjustment_date`:** the column stays; the v4.9.0 code does not know the field and ignores it. Harmless.
- **`Card Cash Disbursement`:** the old code removes the DocType record (`remove_orphan_doctypes`); the table and its
  rows are kept (reasoned, not run). Payments recorded under v4.10.0 are not visible in the old code.
- **Journal Entry hooks:** removed with the code. A Journal Entry posted for a cash payment could then be cancelled
  without the cancellation request. Do not roll back after posting has been enabled without the owner's review.
- **Cash rows:** the old code does not know `is_cash_disbursement`, so a Draft card saved by the old code would count
  a cash row again in its totals. Before a rollback, the owner decides how to treat Draft cards with cash rows.
- **Patches:** nothing is re-run or reversed; Patch Log rows stay.
- **Fixtures:** the v4.9.0 workspace returns on the old code's migrate.
- **Accounting:** the rollback writes no GL entry, Purchase Invoice, Sales Invoice or Journal Entry.

**Rollback procedure (owner approval required for each step):** list the Draft cards with cash rows and the cash
payments first; stop the site; check out `660633b`; run `bench migrate` once; verify submitted-card amounts and posting
are unchanged. A database restore is allowed only when no transaction was made after the backup; otherwise the
rollback is a forward fix.
