## 3. Added — Verified

- **FR-175 — Equipment Supplier Statement for a date range.** Interim settlement every few days from draft cards:
  work of the dates only, payments of the dates (submitted entries only), the balance carried from before and the
  current due, with the owner-approved rule: a CARD posting is not counted twice and the VAT of CARD purchase invoices
  is added. Verified by CI tests (`test_supplier_statement_range.py`).

## 4. Improved — Verified

- **FR-174 — Monthly Card print:** four settlement totals per billed side; Cash paid rows left out of the printed sums
  as on the server (PR #286).

## 5. Fixed — Verified (CI); Desk not yet seen

- **Monthly Card form amount.** The form used the rounded 2-decimal hourly rate (53.13) and could show 6,271.77 while
  the server stored 6,271.13; it now uses the exact rate (53.125), and a saved card opens without being marked changed.

## 9. Breaking Changes — Verified

None. Without the two dates the supplier statement returns the same rows and summary keys as v4.10.1. One date alone
is refused with a message (new filters, no earlier caller).

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- Supplier invoices booked outside CARD count as other credits and are flagged for reconciliation; they are never
  matched to card work automatically.
- With a project/equipment/type filter, payments, VAT and other credits stay the whole supplier's (noted on the
  statement).
- A partial range of a card rounds to the halala: the parts of one card can differ from the card amount by 0.01.
- Not verified on Production: the owner's acceptance figures and the form on a real Desk (section 12).

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.10.1:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate` (no patch; reloads the two standard print
  formats); `bench --site <site> clear-cache`; restart. No `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/` change).
- **What migrate writes:** the two standard Print Format records from their JSON. No document, amount, GL entry,
  invoice, payment or permission.
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner, read-only):** run the supplier statement with the owner's dates and compare the
  figures; open draft monthly cards and check the amount matches and the form is not marked changed. No save, submit or
  cancel.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Target v4.10.1 (`d58f6b7`), code only; v4.10.1 runs on
  the same data. Its migrate reloads its own print formats.
