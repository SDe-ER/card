## 5. Fixed — Partially Verified

- **Issue 320 — the Company settings row editor opens.** A table inside a child row stopped the Desk row editor
  (the Desk form supports a table one level deep only). The table is now on Card Settings; each row names its
  company. Verified by CI tests and from the frappe source; not opened in a browser before the release.

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- Stored threshold rows are not checked by the upgrade. If a site has rows that break the duplicate rule, the next
  save of Card Settings is refused until the row is corrected in the form.
- Not seen on a real Desk before the release (source and CI tests only).
- Everything listed for v4.12.0 that this page does not change.

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.12.0:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate`; `bench --site <site> clear-cache`; restart. No
  `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/` change).
- **What migrate writes:** the DocType records of Card Settings and Card Company Settings (the moved field). **It
  does not write:** any column, document, amount, GL entry, role or permission; no Accounting Dimension.
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner):** open Card Settings, open a row of Company settings with the edit button,
  and check that the three switches of v4.12.0 are off.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Code only, to v4.12.0. Threshold rows saved from the
  new table stay and still apply at submit; the previous form cannot show them.
