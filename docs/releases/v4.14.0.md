## 3. Added — Partially Verified

- **FR-194 — link an existing customer Sales Invoice to its monthly cards.** For a Sales Invoice that an accountant
  booked by hand in ERPNext for work that CARD monthly cards record. The method writes one GL Posting Log (origin
  `Linked`) with one source row per card, sets `sales_invoice`, `posted` and `gl_posting` on each card, and adds an
  Info comment on the invoice and on each card (who, when, reason).
  - **Who:** Accounts Manager or System Manager, with the company in scope, read on the invoice, the customer and
    every card, and the submit right on Sales Invoice.
  - **Checks:** the invoice is submitted, in the company currency, not a return, debit note or opening invoice, has
    no submitted credit note, and is not already a CARD posting or linked; every card is submitted, of a closed
    month, billed to the same customer, company and project, and not invoiced or held; the invoice net (before VAT)
    equals the customer net of the cards, in total and for each project, within `reconciliation_tolerance`.
  - **Not changed:** the invoice (it is never saved), the GL, the Payment Ledger, tax rows, card amounts, rates,
    days, adjustments and workflow states. No ZATCA step runs.
- **List of candidates.** `unlinked_customer_invoice_candidates` returns, per company, the submitted Sales Invoices
  of customers with customer-billing cards that CARD did not build and that have no link, with the unlinked cards of
  the same projects and whether a tested set of cards covers the invoice. It writes nothing.

Verified by CI tests. Not run on a Desk, and not run on a site with `ksa_compliance`, before the release.

## 4. Improved — Partially Verified

- **FR-193 — Project Invoice Profitability.** Project expenses holds only the Purchase Invoice lines that are booked
  to an Expense account (`root_type` = Expense) and are neither a stock item nor a fixed asset. The new column
  **Purchases not in expenses** holds the net amount of the other purchase lines; it is information only and is not
  in the margin. The two columns add up to the net total of the purchase invoice lines.

## 5. Fixed — Partially Verified

- **Issue 324 — the PDF button of the report print page.** On frappe 16.60 the button users see is the one of the
  report print page; for a tree report it sent the browser HTML to wkhtmltopdf and never reached the server route of
  v4.12.2. The two statement scripts now route that button to their server PDF routes. The click path was tried on a
  live Desk session with the download call intercepted; it has no automated test.

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- **No method removes a customer link.** Cancelling the linked Sales Invoice frees the cards; a company under ZATCA
  cannot cancel a submitted Sales Invoice, so a wrong link there needs a reviewed data correction by an
  administrator.
- A Sales Invoice with no project at all (no header project and no line project) cannot be linked.
- The link has no Desk button: it is called as a whitelisted method.
- `link_supplier_invoice` does not have the locking re-reads of the customer method yet.
- Project Invoice Profitability: a stock item is never in Project expenses, also when the stock was consumed on the
  project, so the margin can be higher than the real one. The column Purchases not in expenses shows what was left
  out.
- The PDF of the two statements is the fixed A4 page of the server route: the letter head and the other choices of
  the print page do not apply to it.
- Everything listed for v4.13.0 that this page does not change.

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.13.0:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate`; `bench --site <site> clear-cache`; restart. No
  `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/` change; report scripts are served from the app folder). The compiled
  translations must be rebuilt as for every release with new messages.
- **What migrate writes:** nothing of this release (no DocType, patch, fixture or print format change). **It does not
  write:** any column, document, amount, GL entry, role or permission; no Accounting Dimension.
- **No link is made on upgrade.** A link is a separate, explicit call by an Accounts Manager or a System Manager.
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner):** open Project Invoice Profitability for a period and see the new column;
  open Equipment Customer Statement, use Print, then the PDF button, and check the Arabic words.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Code only, to v4.13.0. Links already made stay and
  are read like every posting log.
