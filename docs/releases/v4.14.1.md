# CARD v4.14.1 — 2026-11-26

> Patch release on top of v4.14.0. Two fixes: the Card workspace draws all its number cards again (FR-168), and
> every locale translates the navigation layer. One addition: a System Manager can remove a wrong link between a
> customer Sales Invoice and its monthly cards (FR-195). One changed rule: the `posted` flag of a card stays 1 when
> a submitted posting log holds the other side without an invoice link on the card. No patch, no schema change, no
> fixture change, no role or DocPerm change; CARD posts no accounting entry.
> **Published 2026-11-26** (tag `v4.14.1`, owner approval 2026-11-26). **Published ≠ deployed** (see
> [DEPLOYMENTS.md](DEPLOYMENTS.md)).
> Labels: **Verified / Partially Verified / Unknown / Not Supported / Deprecated / Removed**.

## 3. Added — Partially Verified

- **FR-195 — unlink a wrong customer invoice link.** For a link made with `link_customer_invoice` (FR-194) that is
  wrong. Before, the link was removed only when the Sales Invoice was cancelled, and a company under ZATCA cannot
  cancel a submitted Sales Invoice.
  - **Who:** System Manager only (an Accounts Manager is refused), with read on the Sales Invoice, the company of
    the link in scope, and read on every card of the link. The System Manager role alone gives no read on Sales
    Invoice, so the caller also needs an accounts role. A reason is mandatory.
  - **Works on:** a submitted Sales Invoice that is the posting document of an active GL Posting Log with origin
    `Linked` (customer side). A posting that CARD built and a CARD credit note are always refused.
  - **Checks, all before the first write:** the log names this invoice; no amendment chain mismatch; every source
    is a submitted monthly card that is marked posted and points at this invoice.
  - **Effect:** on each card `sales_invoice` is cleared, `posted` is set to 0 (1 stays when the supplier side is
    still posted) and `gl_posting` is cleared when it points at this log. The log is set to Cancelled, its
    `posting_key` is cleared, and its `remarks` get who, when, the reason and the cards with their amounts. The log
    is never deleted. One Info comment is added on the invoice and on each card.
  - **Not changed:** the invoice (it is never saved, cancelled or amended), the GL, the Payment Ledger, tax rows,
    card amounts, `modified` of the cards. No ZATCA record is touched.
  - **Link again:** `link_customer_invoice` accepts an invoice whose earlier link was removed. A new log is written;
    the first log stays Cancelled.

Verified by CI tests. Not run on a Desk, and not run on a site with `ksa_compliance`, before the release.

## 4. Improved — Verified

- **Locales: the navigation layer is translated in every language (follow-up of FR-168).** 41 locales get the titles
  of the 9 module sidebars, their links and the Number Card and Dashboard Chart names (60 entries each; 53 in `bn`,
  `hi`, `ur`). `ar.po`: 36 empty entries are filled and 78 missing source strings are added. `bn.po`, `hi.po` and
  `ur.po` translate the 57 Arabic texts that showed in English. The label "Loads last month (CARD)" keeps "(CARD)"
  in 42 locales. Display only. Verified by `card/tests/test_locale_navigation_layer.py`, which reads the files and
  needs no site. Not checked on a Desk in each language.

## 5. Fixed — Partially Verified

- **FR-168 — the hidden number cards of the Card workspace show again.** Frappe draws a number card block only when
  the label of its row equals the translated record name. Since v4.10.0 the two texts differed: the Desk did not
  draw 4 of the 9 number cards in Arabic and all 9 in English. Every `card/locale/*.po` now translates each record
  name as the label of its row. `card/fixtures/workspace.json` and the Number Card records are not changed.
  Verified by `card/tests/test_workspace_number_card_labels.py`, which reads the fixture and the locale files. Not
  checked on a Desk.
- **The `posted` flag when the other side has no invoice link.** `_other_side_still_posted` now also reads the
  submitted posting log of the other side. Case: a sub-rental card whose supplier side is a linked accrual Journal
  Entry (`link_accrual_journal` sets `posted` without a purchase invoice). The cancel of the customer invoice of
  such a card, linked or CARD-built, cleared `posted`; it now stays 1. The same function serves the unlink.
  Verified by CI tests.

## 9. Breaking Changes — Verified

None. Impact by area:

- **Permissions:** one new POST method, System Manager only. No role, DocPerm or DocType permission change.
- **Accounting:** none. No GL Entry, no Payment Ledger Entry, no tax row. CARD posts nothing.
- **Data:** the unlink writes the three link columns of the cards of the log (`sales_invoice`, `posted`,
  `gl_posting`), `status`, `posting_key` and `remarks` of the log, and Comment rows. No schema change.
- **API:** one new method. `link_customer_invoice` now accepts an invoice whose earlier link was removed.
- **Reports:** after an unlink the customer reports show the cards as not invoiced and the invoice as outside CARD.
- **Display:** a DocType or report of the sidebars shows its translated name wherever Frappe shows the name.

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- The link and the unlink have no Desk button: they are called as whitelisted methods.
- The caller of the unlink needs the System Manager role and read on Sales Invoice. A System Manager without an
  accounts role is refused.
- The supplier link and the accrual Journal Entry link have no unlink method.
- `card/locale/main.pot` is older than the source. It has the FR-195 messages, but it was not generated again from
  the code. The generation with the bench i18n flow is a separate task.
- Locales other than English and Arabic, such as Urdu, Hindi and Bengali, carry the navigation layer only. The
  forms and messages stay English and Arabic.
- The unlink is tested in CI without `ksa_compliance`.
- The capture fixes of pull request #343 are proven only by the next approved capture run.
- Everything listed for v4.14.0 that this page does not change. The v4.14.0 limit "No method removes a customer
  link" is closed by FR-195.

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.14.0:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate`; `bench --site <site> clear-cache`; restart. No
  `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/` change). The compiled translations must be rebuilt, as for every
  release with new messages: the number card fix and the menu translations reach a site only with them.
- **Checked from source:** `git diff v4.14.0..HEAD -- card/patches.txt card/patches '*.json' card/hooks.py
  card/fixtures` shows one file, `website/scripts/screenshots/shots.json` (documentation tooling). No patch, no
  DocType JSON, no fixture and no hook changed. The application changes are three Python files and the locale
  files.
- **What migrate writes:** nothing of this release (no DocType, patch, fixture or print format change). **It does not
  write:** any column, document, amount, GL entry, role or permission. No Workspace record is written, so a
  workspace customised on a site is not touched.
- **No unlink is made on upgrade.** An unlink is a separate, explicit call by a System Manager.
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner):** reload the Desk once, open the Card workspace in Arabic and in English and
  count nine number cards.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Code only, to v4.14.0, and compile the locale files
  again. No data step.
  - An unlink made on v4.14.1 stays. To v4.14.0 code it reads as a Cancelled posting log with an empty
    `posting_key` and cards with empty link fields. v4.14.0 writes the same state when a linked Sales Invoice is
    cancelled (`card.gl.events.before_cancel`) and reads only active logs as holders, so the cards show as not
    invoiced and the invoice as outside CARD.
  - One difference: v4.14.0 `link_customer_invoice` refuses to link that invoice again, because its
    `validate_builder_insert` stops at every existing log of the invoice. The link needs v4.14.1 code.
  - On v4.14.0 code the cancel of a customer invoice clears `posted` again on a card whose supplier side is held
    only by a linked accrual Journal Entry.
