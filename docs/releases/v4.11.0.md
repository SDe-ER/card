# CARD v4.11.0 — 2026-11-22

> Minor release on top of v4.10.1: the Equipment Supplier Statement for any date range with payments and the balance
> due (FR-175, PR #287), supplier payments linked to an equipment through the Card Equipment accounting dimension
> (FR-176, PRs #289 and #290), the Monthly Card form shows the server's amount (FR-175), and four clear settlement
> totals on the printed Monthly Card (FR-174, PR #286). Backward compatible: one patch (the accounting dimension:
> empty columns); no DocType, permission or accounting change; one new read-only GET method.
> **Published 2026-11-22** (tag `v4.11.0`). **Published ≠ deployed** (see [DEPLOYMENTS.md](DEPLOYMENTS.md)).
> Labels: **Verified / Partially Verified / Unknown / Not Supported / Deprecated / Removed**.

## 3. Added — Verified

- **FR-175 — Equipment Supplier Statement for a date range.** Interim settlement every few days from draft cards:
  work of the dates only, payments of the dates (submitted entries only), the balance carried from before and the
  current due, with the owner-approved rule: a CARD posting is not counted twice and the VAT of CARD purchase invoices
  is added. Verified by CI tests (`test_supplier_statement_range.py`).

- **FR-176 — Supplier payments linked to an equipment.** The app creates the official ERPNext Accounting Dimension
  Card Equipment; with an Equipment/Project filter the statements deduct only the payments that carry it, allocate
  CARD invoice credit and VAT by line share and show untagged payments as «not deducted». Verified by CI tests
  (`test_card_equipment_dimension.py`, `test_supplier_statement_range.py`).

## 4. Improved — Verified

- **FR-174 — Monthly Card print:** four settlement totals per billed side; Cash paid rows left out of the printed sums
  as on the server (PR #286).

## 5. Fixed — Verified (CI); Desk not yet seen

- **Monthly Card form amount.** The form used the rounded 2-decimal hourly rate (53.13) and could show 6,271.77 while
  the server stored 6,271.13; it now uses the exact rate (53.125), and a saved card opens without being marked changed.

## 9. Breaking Changes — Verified

None. Without the two dates and without an Equipment/Project filter the supplier statement returns the same rows
and summary keys as v4.10.1. With an Equipment/Project filter its ledger lines now follow the entries' equipment and
project (owner decision 2026-11-22). One date alone is refused with a message (new filters, no earlier caller).

## 10. Known Limitations — Verified

- Supplier invoices booked outside CARD count as other credits and are flagged for reconciliation; they are never
  matched to card work automatically.
- Payments recorded before the dimension carry no equipment: with an Equipment filter they show as not allocated
  until they are re-issued with the equipment.
- After an uninstall the dimension's Link fields point at the deleted Card Equipment DocType (accepted; not planned).
- A partial range of a card rounds to the halala: the parts of one card can differ from the card amount by 0.01.
- Not verified on Production: the owner's acceptance figures and the form on a real Desk (section 12).

## 12. Installation & Upgrade — Partially Verified

- **Upgrade from v4.10.1:** new code; `bench --site <site> migrate` (runs the dimension patch once; reloads the two
  standard print formats); `bench --site <site> clear-cache`; restart. No `bench build` (no `public/` or `frontend/`
  change).
- **What migrate writes:** one Accounting Dimension with a non-mandatory row per company, ERPNext's standard empty
  `card_equipment` custom field and column on each accounting doctype (ALTER TABLE per table), and the two standard
  Print Format records. No document, amount, GL entry, invoice, payment or permission.
- **Post-deployment acceptance (owner, read-only):** run the supplier statement with the owner's dates and compare the
  figures; open draft monthly cards and check the amount matches and the form is not marked changed. No save, submit or
  cancel.
- **Safe rollback — Verified from source; not runtime-tested.** Target v4.10.1 (`d58f6b7`), code only; v4.10.1 runs on
  the migrated data (its posting code already uses the dimension when it exists). The dimension and its columns stay;
  the print formats keep the v4.11.0 template until they are reloaded from the v4.10.1 JSON. A database restore
  removes the dimension and every change made after the backup.
