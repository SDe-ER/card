## What CARD does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Monthly operating cards" icon="document">
		One card per machine, project and month: daily hours, stoppages with their reasons and responsible party,
		additions and deductions, and the supplier and customer totals.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Rentals in every direction" icon="random">
		Owned, rented-in, rented-out and sub-let equipment, each priced by the day, hour or month with the day billing
		basis you agreed.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Diesel, transport and meters" icon="rocket">
		Metered diesel sessions allocated by working hours, equipment transport with cost responsibility, and engine-hour
		readings reconciled against the cards.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Standard ERPNext accounting" icon="approve-check">
		CARD never writes ledger entries itself. It prepares draft Purchase and Sales Invoices from submitted cards, by
		company and party, with posting checks.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Statements and reports" icon="list-format">
		Supplier and customer statements in A4, settlement status, downtime, diesel analysis, partial margin and more —
		25 reports in total.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Arabic and English" icon="translate">
		Every screen, print and report in Arabic (right to left) and English, with the app named CARD in every language.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
